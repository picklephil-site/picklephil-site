🧠 Where do the games come from?

This page doesn't host any commercial ROMs — you load game files from your own device and they run locally in your browser (nothing is uploaded anywhere). Backups of cartridges you own are your business; and there's a whole world of free, legal homebrew games made by modern devs for these consoles:

  • itch.io — tons of free NES / SNES / Game Boy homebrew (search "NES rom" or "Game Boy rom")
  • PDRoms — a classic archive of legal homebrew & public-domain ROMs for basically every console
  • Homebrew Hub — hundreds of community-made Game Boy games
  • 🎮 Plug in a USB / Bluetooth controller — it just works (press a button so the browser sees it).
  • 💾 Your save states live in this browser, per game.
  • 📱 On a phone, rotate to landscape for the full arcade feel.
